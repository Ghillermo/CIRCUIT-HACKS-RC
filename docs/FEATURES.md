## Glossary

Every feature of the mod under the groups of the former feature list (A-E). Each title leads to its section; next to it, its view chapter.

### A. Shortcuts and usability

- **[A1 Shortcut map](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-atajos)** — Every shortcut of the mod on a drawing of the device: double presses, Shift + button, Clear + knob and the special pads of each view. *View:* [0.2 Buttons that work in every view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#botones)
- **[A2 Master FX on the Mixer button](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-master-fx-acceso)** — From the Mixer, **Mixer again** opens Master FX; any other view button leaves it. *View:* [6.1 Master FX](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#master-fx)
- **[A3 Keys of the FX view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-vista-fx)** — **Octave ▲ / ▼** = delay / reverb sends; **Shift + knob** = reverb and delay parameters. **Duplicate** = reset (sends to 0, master filter centred, Master FX off); **Shift + Octave ▲ / ▼** = master filter resonance ±16 (since RC4; before, **1-16 / 17-32**); **Shift + pad 25** = master filter off / on (since RC4). *View:* [7.1 FX view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#vista-fx)
- **[A4 Clear + knob = default value](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-clear-knob)** — Hold **Clear** and turn a knob right: it goes back to its default. The LED turns **lavender** (the original's blue). On the drums, turned left it deletes the automation of the stock knobs (red LED), as on the original. Also on the MIDI track engine and in **Master FX**. *View:* [0.2 Buttons that work in every view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#botones)
- **[A5 Switch off latched effects (Master FX pad 8)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-pad8)** — **Pad 8** switches off everything latched with Shift (effects and song pointer) and bypasses the FX; touching a latched effect releases it. *View:* [6.1 Master FX](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#master-fx)
- **[A6 Synth source view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-fuente-sinte)** — With Synth 1 / 2 on screen, **press its button again**: pads 25-29 pick the source (noise, inputs, inverted inputs), pad 30 = step-drummer. *View:* [2.3 Synth source view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#synth-fuente)
- **[A7 Chromatic and scale view of the drums](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-cromatica)** — **Shift + Preset** with a drum: the pads play the sample in the scale; K1 = pitch offset, Shift + K1 = cents; Note view: pads 25-28 = drums 4-1. *View:* [5.3 Chromatic and scale view of the drums](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#drum-cromatica)
- **[A8 Pack management on the device](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-pack)** — **Save** also writes the samples replaced by captures to the card. **Duplicate + pad A + pad B** (Drum Preset) swaps two samples; between packs it **copies** samples and synth patches; **Clear**, the pack, **Clear** (packs view) deletes a pack. *View:* [5.2 Drum Preset view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#drum-preset)
- **[A9 Replace a drum with the capture](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-sustituir)** — Drum Preset view: **Clear + pad twice** = that sample becomes the capture (until you save or reload). *View:* [5.2 Drum Preset view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#drum-preset)
- **[A10 CC editor of the MIDI macros](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-editor-cc)** — MIDI 1 / 2 Preset view, **pad 25**: move a knob and change its CC / NRPN on the device; pad 26 = save. *View:* [3.7 CC editor of the MIDI macros (pad 25)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#midi-cc)
- **[A11 Mixer: Shift + knob = level to 100 / 0](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-mixer-shift)** — Mixer view: **Shift + a knob turned right** sets that track’s level to **100**; **turned left**, to **0**. *View:* [9.2 Mixer view and scenes](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#mixer)
- **[A12 Cue mode, mutes on the bar and quantize (since RC2)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-mixer-cue)** — **Since RC2.** Mixer, pan page: **Shift + knob** = cue mode (the right output listens to the tracks you choose). **Shift + pads 1-8** of the Mixer = mute / unmute on the next bar. **Shift + Play** = quantize the pattern of the selected track. *View:* [9.2 Mixer view and scenes](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#mixer)
- **[A13 LFO synced to the tempo (since RC4)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-lfo-sync)** — **Since RC4.** The LFO rate knobs are bipolar: right = a free LFO, left = synced to the tempo with the cycle on the bar's first beat. *View:* [0.2 Buttons that work in every view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#botones)
- **[A14 Split keyboard Synth 1 + Synth 2](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-teclado-partido)** — **Since RC2.** Hold **Synth 1** and press **Synth 2** (or the other way round): the two top rows play the synth you held first and the two bottom rows the other one; **since RC2.02**, any pair of Synth 1, Synth 2, MIDI 1 and MIDI 2. *View:* [2.1 Synth 1 / 2 track view (Note, Velocity, Gate)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#synth-nota)
- **[A15 Synth patch colours](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-colores-patch)** — **From RC2 to RC4.08** every patch of the Synth 1 / 2 Preset view was drawn by its voice mode. **Since RC4.10** the colours are the original's: those card reads sometimes loaded the initial patch. **Beta 5.65 only:** each patch in the colour of its **Components category**. *View:* [2.4 Synth 1 / 2 Preset view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#synth-preset)
- **[A16 Patches of the own synth (MIDI 1 / 2)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-patches-midi)** — **Since RC2.** In the Preset view of MIDI 1 / 2, rows 2 and 3 are 16 patch slots of the track engine: touch = load (the root note sounds), **Clear + pad** twice = save, **Duplicate + A + B** = exchange; **Octave ▼ / ▲** = the pages (**since RC4**: 8 pages, 128 slots per pack; before, 4 pages, 64 slots). *View:* [3.1 MIDI 1 / 2 Preset view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#midi-preset)
- **[A17 Saving the synth’s patch into the pack](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-guardar-patch)** — **Since RC2.** In the Preset view of Synth 1 / 2: **Clear + pad** asks (it blinks white) and **Clear + the same pad again** saves the patch as it sounds into that slot. *View:* [2.4 Synth 1 / 2 Preset view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#synth-preset)
- **[A18 Macro knob destinations](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-destinos-macro)** — **Since RC2.** In the synth source view (A6), **pad 32** opens the editor: a knob picks its macro, **Octave ▼ / ▲** steps the field and the pads draw the number. *View:* [2.3 Synth source view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#synth-fuente)
- **[A19 Copying projects to another slot or another pack](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-copiar-proyectos)** — **Since RC2.** In the Projects view: **Duplicate + pad A** picks the project (it blinks yellow) and **Duplicate + pad B** copies it into that slot, of the same pack or of another one. *View:* [10.1 Projects view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#projects)
- **[A20 Own-synth patches to another pack](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-patches-entre-packs)** — **Since RC2.** In the Preset view of MIDI 1 / 2: **Duplicate + slot A**, load another pack and **Duplicate + slot B** copies the patch (engine, parameters and name). *View:* [3.1 MIDI 1 / 2 Preset view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#midi-preset)
- **[A21 Shift = a flash on every note (Beta 5.65 only)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#f-luces-disparo)** — With Shift held, each track's button flashes white on every note it plays. *View:* [0.2 Buttons that work in every view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#botones)
- **[A22 Shift + Preset = the project's sound (Beta 5.65 only)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#f-proyecto-init)** — Shift + Preset brings the track's sound back to the one saved in the project. *View:* [0.2 Buttons that work in every view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#botones)

### B. Exposed controls

- **[B1 Drum knobs as printed on the panel](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-knobs-drums)** — Every knob does what its label says, with a second layer on Shift: ring mod, envelopes, filters (DJ, BP, notch, comb (not in Beta 5.65), tilt), LFO, start point, bitcrush and distortion. *View:* [5.1 Drum track view (knobs)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#drum-pista)
- **[B2 Reverb and delay parameters](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-rev-delay)** — FX view, **Shift + knob**: reverb (type, decay, damping) and delay (time, sync, feedback, width, ratio, slew); saved with the project. *View:* [7.1 FX view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#vista-fx)
- **[B3 Side-chain and master compressor on the knobs](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-sidechain-comp)** — **Since RC2.** In the Sidechain view (**Shift + FX**): **K1-K4** = attack, hold, decay and depth of the side-chain of the chosen track; **K7 / K8** = attack and release of the master compressor. *View:* [8.1 Sidechain view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#sidechain)
- **[B4 Recording the internal MIDI routes](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-grabar-interno)** — **Since RC2.** In the Setup view, **Record** decides whether what a MIDI track sends to an internal synth over a shared channel is also recorded into that synth: **yellow = yes**, dark = no (the default). *View:* [1.1 Setup view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#setup)
- **[B5 MIDI for everything new](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-midi-io)** — The new values go out and come in over MIDI: CC / NRPN of the MIDI track engine, NRPN of the drums (MSB 5-8) and of Master FX (MSB 9, channel 16). *View:* [11.4 MIDI: own behaviours, new CCs and NRPNs](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#midi)
- **[B6 Automation of the MIDI engine](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-automatizacion)** — With Record + Play, the 8 values of page 4 of the MIDI engine are recorded into the macro lanes of the pattern. *View:* [3.3 Knob pages of the MIDI 1 / 2 engine](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#midi-knobs)
- **[B7 Setup and device MIDI](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-setup)** — Channel 16 Rx / Tx (pad 16), shared channels, **internal loopback** without a cable and **reverb / delay returns through strips 3 / 4** (pads 22 / 23, cream / peach like the reverb and delay of the FX view). **since RC2:** MIDI Thru with four modes (Duplicate in Advanced Setup): Thru, Out 2, **Merge** (DIN In + the device’s MIDI) and **Filter** (DIN In without the channels the device listens to). **since RC2:** own **Program Change** channels: pads 29 / 30 show and pick the listen / output channel (16 by default); what is received is forwarded on the output one. **since RC2:** **metronome with count-in**: Shift + Clear walks no metronome, metronome, count-in only and both; the count-in is one bar before recording. **since RC4:** **General MIDI mode of the drums**: in Setup, K4 turns it on; K5-K8 = the GM note of Drum 1-4 (35-81), sent on the drums' channel instead of 60 / 62 / 64 / 65. *View:* [1.1 Setup view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#setup)
- **[B8 Patch flip on MIDI 1 / 2](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-patch-flip)** — Pad 26 of the MIDI Preset view: every note is sent as a Program Change (exclusive with the track engine). *View:* [3.1 MIDI 1 / 2 Preset view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#midi-preset)

### C. New control modes

- **[C1 Patch morph (Beta 5.65 only)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#f-morph)** — **Beta 5.65 only.** In the Preset view of Synth 1 / 2 or MIDI 1 / 2 (own synth, with its 8-operator FM): **hold two to four patches** for 0.4 s and a knob moves from one to the next, with LFOs, an envelope and a slow morph. *View:* [2.4 Synth 1 / 2 Preset view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#synth-preset)
- **[C2 Engine of the MIDI tracks (pad 27)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-motor-midi)** — **Since RC2.08:** an external keyboard on the channel of MIDI 1 / 2 (USB or DIN) plays its own synth; its notes are not sent again to the MIDI out. Pad 27 of the MIDI 1 / 2 Preset view picks the engine: off, **line**, **oscillator**, **VA-drum**, **granular**, **sample** (since RC2), **FX sequencer** and, **since RC4**, **FM**. **Since RC4** a tap opens them as a row on pads 17-24 (tap one to pick it; before, each tap stepped to the next) and Shift + pad 27 steps to the next. Pads 1-5 pick the knob page; 28 = two oscillators, 29 = chain order, 30 = step-drummer, 31 = velocity (VA-drum). **since RC2:** Clear + a page pad = the values of that page back to their initial values; rows 2-3 hold patches (A14). *View:* [3.1 MIDI 1 / 2 Preset view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#midi-preset)
- **[C3 Step-drummer](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-step-drummer)** — Sequencing by **pairs of steps** on top of the pattern: pads 1-16 steps, 17-24 destination / macro, the 8 knobs = the 8 pairs (pitch or velocity). MIDI: Preset view, pad 30. Synths: source view, pad 30. While recording, the steps you play are recorded in real time. *View:* [3.8 Step-drummer view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#step-drummer)
- **[C4 FX sequencer](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-fx-seq)** — **Blue** state of pad 27: the step-drummer of the track picks, for each pair of steps, **a slot of the Master FX bottom row** (0 = none); it plays on the steps with a note. The knob moves slowly (**one slot every ~48 steps of the turn**) and lights in the **colour of the chosen effect**. **A slot with no effect assigned** plays, **since RC4**, the effect at the top of its column (pad 26 = Master FX pad 1 … pad 30 = pad 5), with its knobs; before, it did nothing: assign the effects to the Master FX bottom row (see “Assignable bottom row”). **since RC2:** at the end of the travel there is the **random** value (white LED): on every step with a note one of the list plays at random (the slots with an effect assigned and the mechanical gesture of pad 31). *View:* [3.8 Step-drummer view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#step-drummer)
- **[C5 Song pointer (Master FX)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-song-pointer)** — Pads 9-24 = steps 1-16: one held step = retrigger; two steps = momentary phrase. **Phrase mode** (a latched phrase): Shift + step = start (end 16); a touch = the end; Shift + another step = new start; Shift + the start or the white pad 9 = exit. *View:* [6.1 Master FX](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#master-fx)
- **[C6 Captured sample](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-sample)** — **Pad 32** (red) records the output; **pad 7** (yellow) plays it: K1 pitch, K2 mode (one-shot / gate / loop by colour), K3 start, K4 end. The sample can be assigned to the bottom row (pad 7 + a bottom pad) and can replace a drum (A8). *View:* [6.1 Master FX](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#master-fx)
- **[C7 Glitch (pad 6) and its assignment](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-glitch-pad)** — **Hold pad 6** of Master FX: the output breaks up and lags like a tape. K1 speed, K2 repeat, K3 delay, K4 chaos (lower half) or overload (upper half). **Pad 6 held + a bottom-row pad** = that slot is a Glitch too, with its copy of K1-K4. *View:* [6.1 Master FX](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#master-fx)
- **[C8 Assignable bottom row (Master FX / FX)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-fila-inferior)** — Hold an effect of the top rows (or pad 7, or pad 31) and touch a bottom pad: that slot stores the effect with a copy of its parameters. Saved with the project. *View:* [6.1 Master FX](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#master-fx)
- **[C9 Random-mutate per step](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-random-mutate)** — **Since RC2.** In the probability view, in mode 2 (**pad 25** of Pattern Settings), row 4 gives each step a chance of its notes jumping an octave or moving to another note of the scale when they play. *View:* [4.3 Probability view (note tracks and drums)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#probabilidad)
- **[C10 Step frequency and dynamics](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-frecuencia-dinamicas)** — **Since RC2.** In the probability view of the note tracks: **row 4 = every how many plays of the pattern the step sounds** (1-8). With **pad 25 of Pattern Settings**: row 3 = random **dynamics** (velocity, position and length), row 4 = random-mutate. *View:* [4.3 Probability view (note tracks and drums)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#probabilidad)
- **[C11 Pattern chains and scenes](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-cadenas)** — **Since RC2.** Every pattern can carry a rule “play n times, then jump to B; the next time, to C”. **Shift + Octave ▲ / ▼** in the Patterns view switches the **chain mode** of the whole project on / off; every scene remembers the mode it was saved in. *View:* [9.1 Patterns view and pattern chains](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#patterns)
- **[C12 Pattern mutate (Beta 5.65 only)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#f-mutate-patrones)** — **Beta 5.65 only.** Ported from the Pattern Mutate in [mefisme's Rhythm Plus](https://github.com/mefisme/rhythm-plus) (MIT licence). In the Patterns view or the Mixer, **Shift + a tap on Duplicate** gives each selected track a short list of patterns from its chain, in another order, that loops in place of the chain. The patterns themselves don't change. *View:* [9.1 Patterns view and pattern chains](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#patterns)
- **[C13 Nudge and play orders](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-nudge-ordenes)** — **Since RC2.** In the Pattern Settings view: **pad 26 = Nudge** (moves the content of the selected steps), **pad 27 = fractal order**, **pad 28 = deconstructed order**. On every track, drums included. *View:* [4.1 Pattern Settings view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#pattern-settings)
- **[C14 Arpeggiator (since RC2)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-arpegiador)** — **Since RC2.** In the Pattern Settings view of a note track: **Shift + pad 25** = the pattern's arpeggiator on / off, **Shift + pads 27-32** = its order (fractal, deconstructed, up, down, ping-pong, random), independent of the pattern's direction. *View:* [4.1 Pattern Settings view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#pattern-settings)
- **[C15 Drums micro-step rollers (since RC2)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-rollers)** — **Since RC2.** Rows 1-3 of the drums' Note view: holding a roller plays its drum on every step at that micro step (two columns per drum, micro steps 1-3 and 4-6), live; with the transport stopped, at the project's tempo. *View:* [5.3 Chromatic and scale view of the drums](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#drum-cromatica)
- **[C16 Custom scale (since RC2)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-escala-propia)** — **Since RC2.** **Shift + Scales** = an editor of a custom scale for the note tracks: up to 7 notes over the root and any **period** (up to 24 semitones) that is the root of the next iteration; every row of pads is one iteration (two with 4 notes or fewer). Saved with the project. *View:* [2.2 Scales view and custom scale](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#escalas)
- **[C17 Chord screen (since RC4)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-acordes)** — **Since RC4.** In the **expanded Note view** of Synth 1 / 2 and MIDI 1 / 2, **Shift + pad 8** opens the chord screen: row 1 the type, row 2 the inversion and the modifiers, rows 3-4 the roots; each root plays its chord while it is held. *View:* [2.1 Synth 1 / 2 track view (Note, Velocity, Gate)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#synth-nota)
- **[C18 Stereo drum pairs (since RC4)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-pareja-estereo)** — **Since RC4.** **Hold Drum 1 and press Drum 2** (or Drum 3 + Drum 4): Drum 1 / 3 sounds on the left and Drum 2 / 4 on the right with the next sample, both driven by Drum 1 / 3's controls and pattern; Drum 2 / 4's knobs = stereo width (K2), detune (K1) and deviations. Saved with the project. *View:* [5.1 Drum track view (knobs)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#drum-pista)
- **[C19 The drums' extra lane (Beta 5.65 only)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#f-carril-extra)** — **Beta 5.65 only.** Each drum has **one more automation lane**, kept in the spare bits of each step of its pattern (it travels with Duplicate, Nudge and Shift + Play). From the factory it carries the **chromatic pitch** (the chromatic view's pads record there; the pitch lane stays K1's); **Record held + a knob** assigns another parameter and **Record held + a chromatic pad** brings back the chromatic pitch. *View:* [5.1 Drum track view (knobs)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#drum-pista)
- **[C20 Sample mode of the MIDI tracks (since RC2)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-modo-muestra)** — **Since RC2.** A new state of pad 27 (teal, after the granular): the MIDI track plays **a pack sample one-shot**, at the note's pitch (**C3 = note 60 = as recorded**), through the engine's filter, effect and LFO; the VCA stays open and the sample plays to its end. The **expanded Note view** (Note twice) is the picker: rows 3-4 = 16 samples × 4 pages (Octave ▼ / ▲: all 64 of the pack), rows 1-2 = the steps; **a held step + a sample** = that step's sample flip, also live with Record. *View:* [3.9 Sample mode and sample picker](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#midi-muestra)
- **[C21 Mod matrix of the own synth (since RC2.02)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-matriz-mod)** — **Since RC2.02.** With the MIDI 1 / 2 engine on, **pad 6** of its Preset view is a new knob page: **K1-K8 = eight sources** (velocity, note, random per note, LFO 2, envelope 2, a drum hit, the pattern position, stepped random). Each knob's **position** is the **destination** (every parameter of the knob pages, 33, one stretch each, in its page's colour) and, inside the stretch, the **amount**. Slow knobs: two clicks a step, 16 steps a destination. *View:* [3.4 Mod matrix](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#midi-matriz)
- **[C22 Jump sequencer of MIDI 1 / 2 (Beta 5.65 only)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#f-saltos)** — The notes of a MIDI track make another track's pattern jump to the step they indicate. *View:* [3.1 MIDI 1 / 2 Preset view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#midi-preset)
- **[C23 4-operator FM (since RC4)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-fm4)** — **Since RC4.** A variant of the oscillator of the MIDI 1 / 2 own synth: **pad 24** of the engine row (a tap on pad 27), violet; its knobs on **pad 7**. **K1-K4** = the ratio of operators 1-4 (with a **fine detune** on each ratio's clicks), **K5-K8** = their level; **Shift + K1-K4** = to the right its attack, to the left the operator **percussive** with its own decay; **Shift + K5** = the **algorithm** (16; also by **holding pad 7**: pads 9-24 are the algorithms, a press picks one and plays it), **Shift + K6** feedback, **Shift + K7** noise, **Shift + K8** release (longer with more velocity). *View:* [3.5 4-operator FM](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#midi-fm)
- **[C24 8-operator FM (Beta 5.65 only)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#f-fm8)** — **Beta 5.65 only.** It replaces the 4-operator FM on **pad 24** of the engine row: **up to 8 operators**, each with a **DX7-style envelope** (4 rates, 4 levels), and **56 algorithms** (the 4-operator FM's 16, the DX7's 32 and 8 with eight operators). 4-operator FM patches load converted, with the same sound. FM page = **pad 7** (with the engine row closed): **pads 9-16** = operator 1-8 (the ones the algorithm uses lit: carriers green, modulators light blue), **pad 3** = the pitch envelope (violet). **K1** ratio (0.5 … 31 or fixed in Hz), **K2** fine, **K3** level, **K4** velocity, **K5-K8** rates R1-R4; with Shift **K1** algorithm, **K2** feedback, **K3** rate scaling, **K4** level scaling, **K5-K8** levels L1-L4. *View:* [3.6 8-operator FM](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#midi-fm8)
- **[C25 Wavetable (Beta 5.65 only)](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#f-wavetable)** — **Beta 5.65 only.** **Pad 21** of the engine row (sand) = the oscillator reading a **wavetable**: both oscillators, the FM / ring / sync between them, the filter, the VCA, polyphony and the voice modes stay as they are. **One table per patch, up to 9 frames**, for both oscillators; each its own **position**: **K3** of the OSC 1 / OSC 2 page (WAVE; in Beta 5.24-5.26, K7), K7 = LFO → position, Shift + K3 = envelope → position. **SHAPE** (K2) and envelope / LFO → shape (K4 / K6) = **warp**, a bend of the phase. *View:* [3.1 MIDI 1 / 2 Preset view](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/beta/guide.html#midi-preset)
- **[C26 Master FX since RC2: mechanical gesture, Glitch, Digitise and saving per session](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#f-master-fx-beta)** — **Since RC2.** Pad 6 of Master FX is the **Glitch** and pad 31 a **mechanical gesture** (tape stop, scratch…); Digitise gains **K3 = momentary send to the delay** and **K4 = envelope**; the Master FX knobs and slots **are saved with every project**. *View:* [6.1 Master FX](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc/guide.html#master-fx)
