# CIRCUIT-HACKS

Firmware patches for the **Novation Circuit Tracks** (official firmware 1.2.1, build 4486): a synth engine on the MIDI tracks, new drum controls, Master FX, an arpeggiator, custom scales and chords, pattern chains, polyphony and more. **For Circuit Tracks only**: for the Circuit Rhythm, see [Circuit Remix](https://github.com/Ghillermo/CIRCUIT-REMIX).

Current versions (2026-10-27): **RC2.10**, the safer **RC2.10S** and **Beta 3.37**.

## Start here
- **Install:** download [`rc_uploader.zip`](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/rc_uploader.zip), unzip it, open `rc_uploader.html` in Chrome or Edge and follow the page. **Back up your SD card first.** Step by step in the [FAQ](https://github.com/Ghillermo/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/wiki/FAQ).
- **The manual:** the [guide by views](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/guide.html) (every view, pad and knob), the [guide in plain words](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/plain.html), the [feature list](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/en.html) and the [MIDI reference](https://ghillermo.github.io/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/midi-en.html).
- **What changed:** the [changelog](https://github.com/Ghillermo/CIRCUIT-HACKS-RC/blob/main/docs/CHANGELOG.md#changelog): new features (linked to the guide) and fixes.
